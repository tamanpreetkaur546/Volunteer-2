🌍 VolunteerGateway – Volunteer Registration & Event Management System
🤝 Connecting People. Empowering Communities.
VolunteerGateway is a web-based platform designed to connect volunteers with community service opportunities. It provides a common platform where individuals can explore volunteering activities and organizers can create and manage events.
The main aim of this project is to encourage community participation and make volunteering opportunities easier to discover and access.
---
✨ Features
👤 Volunteer Section
Volunteer registration form.
Volunteer login interface.
Explore available volunteering events.
View event details such as name, date, location, and category.
User-friendly navigation.
🏢 Organizer Section
Separate organizer login interface.
Organizer home page.
Create new volunteering events.
Enter event details, including name, date, address, category, and description.
Organizer information page.
🌱 Community Activities
The platform includes different types of community service activities, such as:
🩸 Blood Donation Camps
💻 Digital Literacy Drives
🍲 Food Distribution Drives
🐾 Animal Welfare Activities
🌳 Cleanliness Drives
❤️ Senior Citizen Care Programs

🎯 Project Objective
The objective of VolunteerGateway is to promote social responsibility and encourage people to contribute their time and skills towards meaningful community activities.
It aims to make volunteering more accessible by bringing volunteers and event organizers onto one platform.
